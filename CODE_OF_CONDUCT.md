# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accountability, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes trolling, insults, personal attacks, harassment, publishing private information without permission, and conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove or reject contributions that do not align with this Code. Report abusive or unacceptable behavior through GitHub's private reporting features. Do not include OAuth credentials or other secrets.
